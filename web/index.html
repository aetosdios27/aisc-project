How to play

Black moves first. Place a disc so that one or more White discs sit between it and another Black disc. Those discs flip to Black. Captures work horizontally, vertically, and diagonally.

The dots mark legal moves. You must pass if you have none. The game ends when neither player can move; the player with more discs wins.

How to interpret these results

Each seeded opening is played twice with the engines’ colors swapped. The same openings are shared across pairings. Confidence intervals resample whole opening pairs, keeping the two color games together.

Equal placement depth controls the search horizon. Equal time measures practical playing strength, including evaluator cost. Both protocols keep legal moves and terminal scoring identical.

Training and validation seeds are separate from tournament seeds. Neural test positions come from separate game groups, with symmetric and near-duplicate states filtered. Prediction error and game strength measure different things.

The saved runs are pilot evidence. The feature freeze is 20 October; 21–24 October is reserved for larger experiments, documentation, presentation, and viva preparation.
