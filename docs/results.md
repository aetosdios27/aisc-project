# Measured pilot results

Generated by `python -m experiments.analyze` from saved JSON. These are preliminary measurements, not a final superiority claim.

The round robin contains 300 games, 20 per opponent pair, at placement depth 2. The primary evaluator pairings each contain ten distinct seeded openings played with both colors. All 324 main/depth-study transcripts replay to their recorded final states.

## Search correctness and efficiency

Across six seeded depth-three positions with positional ordering, Minimax visits 9,356 nodes and Alpha-Beta visits 3,303: 64.7% fewer. Every measured position/depth/ordering combination asserts identical value and selected move.

Score rate counts a draw as half. Intervals resample whole opening pairs 2,000 times. The small opening panel limits interpretation. The GA has no demonstrated gain over manual evaluation in this pilot; both manual and GA perform better than this initial neural model on the measured panel.

## Evolution and neural training

The GA runs 4 generations with population 6. Its selected champion scores 0.50 on the small fixed validation panel. Training fitness uses a changing opponent panel and is not a comparable cross-generation strength estimate.

The neural model has 16,641 parameters. Validation selects epoch 1 of 20. Test MSE is 0.8730, versus 0.9251 for the training-mean predictor; MAE is 0.8842 and Pearson correlation is 0.2429. Training loss continues falling while validation loss worsens: clear overfitting. A prediction improvement does not imply playing-strength improvement.
